ONE LANGUAGE. MANY VOICES. ONE FUTURE.

Translation is no longer only about
moving words from one language to another.

It is about preserving meaning, culture, trust and the human voice in an increasingly intelligent world. This year's edition brings together academics, professionals and students to ask what translation becomes when artificial intelligence enters the room — and where the human translator remains irreplaceable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can attend?
The event is open to translators, interpreters, students, academics and anyone interested in the future of language and translation.
Is the event online or onsite?
The Arabic Edition takes place onsite at Saint Joseph University. Details for the International Edition will be announced separately.
Is registration free?
Registration details, including any fees, will be confirmed on the registration page.
Will there be a certificate?
Yes, all participants completing the event will receive an official certificate of participation.
Will the sessions be in Arabic or English?
The Arabic Edition is conducted primarily in Arabic. The International Edition will be conducted in English.
Who is organizing the event?
The event is organized by Apex Group International in partnership with Saint Joseph University.
